# Message Brief

**Kelompok:** Yasmina Arethaya Hanjani (NIM ________) · Cut Nabilah Nuur Azizah (NIM ________)
**Dataset:** *Earthquakes in Indonesia* (BMKG RepoGempa catalog), Kaggle, doi:10.34740/kaggle/ds/1934565
**Judul:** *Bukan Bumi yang Makin Gaduh, Telinga Kita yang Makin Tajam*

**1. Audience.** The audience is editors and reporters on the science and disaster desks of national online media who write the January "BMKG catat X gempa" story.

- They know magnitude and *gempa dirasakan*.
- They print BMKG's announced total: 29.869 for 2024 under the headline "Gempa Makin Sering" (CNBC Indonesia, 21 Jan 2025), then 43.439 for 2025. They seldom use the M5+ and felt counts in the same releases.
- Few know that a catalog holds only what its network can detect.
- Their headline shapes whether the public thinks risk is rising.

**2. Main message.**

> Jumlah gempa yang diumumkan BMKG naik lima kali lipat dari 2020 ke 2025, tetapi menurut angka BMKG sendiri gempa M5 ke atas justru turun dari 244 menjadi 153, sehingga yang bertambah adalah gempa kecil yang kini lebih banyak terekam, bukan gempa yang lebih kuat.

**3. Supporting claims.**

1. **The rise is small quakes, in BMKG's releases and in its public catalog.**
   - In the releases, the total went 8.264 → 43.439 while M5+ went 244 → 153.
   - The public catalog, which matches BMKG's year-end totals for 2020–2024 within 5%, shows that 97% of its rise from 2014–2017 to 2022–2025 is below M4, while M4+ rose only 10%. *(F1, F2)*
2. **The catalog now records smaller quakes, each measured with more readings, and this is not a labeling artifact.**
   - The most common magnitude fell from M3,4 to M3,0.
   - Median phase readings for M4,0–4,9 quakes rose from 20 to 44.
   - Both shifts hold within a single magnitude type (MLv). *(F3, F4)*
3. **Real surges are short, local and visible in M4+.**
   - The six busiest months since 2009 each trace to a named sequence.
   - 2026 so far runs at ±2.430 M4+ per year, inside the normal range. *(F5, F6)*

**4. Decision to influence** (coverage of the 2026 tally, January 2027):

1. Print BMKG's M5+ and felt counts next to the total; both are already in the release.
2. Write "naik" or "makin sering" only if M5+ also rose.
3. Name the sequence when a spike appears.
4. Base preparedness on the constant baseline (±2.150 M4+ a year, about six a day), not on a rising total.
